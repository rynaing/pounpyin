How it works

Three steps. One unforgettable year.

1

Gift it

You buy Pounpyin for your mom, dad, or grandparent — for Thingyan, a birthday, or no reason at all. We invite them with a warm message in Burmese and English.

2

They answer by voice

Each week a new question arrives where they already are — no apps to learn, no passwords. They tap, talk, and tell it like they'd tell you across the dinner table.

3

A book arrives

At year's end we weave their stories and photos into a beautiful hardcover book — one copy included, more for the whole family. The kind of thing grandchildren fight over, lovingly.

Questions

Everything families ask us

Is it really in Burmese?

Yes — every question is written Burmese-first, then English alongside it. Answers can be spoken in whichever language feels natural; transcription handles both. This is the whole point of Pounpyin.

My parent doesn't use email. Will this work?

That's exactly who we built it for. Questions arrive where your elders already are — messaging apps, not inboxes — and answering is one tap to record. No accounts, no passwords, no typing.

What if they miss a few weeks?

Life happens. Missed prompts wait patiently, and gentle reminders nudge without nagging. The book is laid out from whatever's there at year's end — even twenty stories make a treasure.

Who can see the stories?

Only your family. Stories are private to your family's account, always. Nothing is shared, sold, or made public — the book belongs to you.

What about Karen, Chin, or other languages?

We start with Burmese and English, because that's where we can do it well. Karen and Chin communities are a priority for what's next — many of those stories are the most urgent to preserve.

When does it launch?

We're building now. Join the waitlist and you'll be first to know — founding families help shape the question library.
